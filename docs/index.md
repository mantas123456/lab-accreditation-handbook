# Lab Accreditation Handbook

**A free, practical guide to getting a testing or calibration lab accredited
to ISO/IEC 17025, and keeping it accredited.**

The standard tells you *what* a lab must do. This handbook explains, in plain
English, *how* a small lab can actually do it: what an assessor will ask to
see, practical steps, worked examples with realistic numbers, and the
mistakes that most often become findings.

## How the handbook is organised

One page per clause of ISO/IEC 17025:2017, all with the same headings: in
one sentence; what an assessor looks for; how to do it in a small lab; a
worked example; common findings; templates; tools; related clauses.

| Part | Clauses |
|---|---|
| [4 General](general/4-1-impartiality.md) | Impartiality, confidentiality |
| [5 Structure](structure/5-structure.md) | Legal entity, scope, roles |
| [6 Resources](resources/6-1-resources-overview.md) | People, facilities, equipment, traceability, suppliers |
| [7 Process](process/7-1-contract-review.md) | From contract review to reporting, complaints and data |
| [8 Management system](management/8-1-options.md) | Documents, records, risks, corrective action, audits, review |

The [clause map](clause-map.md) shows every clause with its templates,
tools and status.

## Calculations: labqc

Chapters that need maths link to
[labqc](https://github.com/mantas123456/lab-quality-toolkit), a free Python
library and [web app](https://lab-quality-toolkit-3go33hyzzwjhwpktn8tles.streamlit.app)
for uncertainty budgets, method validation, PT scores and QC charts. The
handbook explains what to do and why; labqc does the numbers.

!!! warning "Not official guidance"
    This handbook is one practitioner's interpretation. It never quotes the
    standard; it cites clause numbers and explains them in its own words.
    The standard itself and your accreditation body's documents always take
    priority.
