# 8.4 Control of records

## In one sentence

Keep the records that prove what you did, readable and findable, protected
from loss and unauthorised change, for as long as you have decided, and
dispose of them in a controlled way.

## What an assessor looks for

- **Records that are legible** and kept as evidence of meeting the
  standard.
- **Controls** for identifying, storing, protecting, backing up, archiving,
  retrieving and disposing of records.
- **Retention periods** that fit contracts, legal requirements and your
  accreditation body's rules.
- **Access** consistent with confidentiality
  ([4.2](../general/4-2-confidentiality.md)).
- **Records that can be found** within a reasonable time. Assessors often
  ask for records from years ago.

## Documents and records are different

| | Document | Record |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | An instruction: how to do something | Evidence: what was done |
| Changes | Revised and re-issued with a new version | Never revised; corrections leave the original readable |
| Example | SOP for moisture | The moisture worksheet for sample 26-0412 |

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **List your record types** and, for each one: where it is kept, who owns
   it, how long it is kept, and how it is disposed of.
2. **Set retention periods** from three sources: contracts, the law, and
   the accreditation body's rules. If none sets a period, choose one that
   covers at least the full accreditation cycle, so an assessor can see
   records since the last full assessment.
3. **Store electronically where you can**, on a server with backups, and
   test a restore once a year.
4. **Scan paper records** into the job file soon after work, or store
   paper in a dry, locked place.
5. **Dispose of records on purpose**: when the period ends, shred or delete,
   and log it.
6. **Protect against change**: read-only archives, audit trails
   ([7.11](../process/7-11-data-management.md)).

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab's records retention table, extract:

| Record | Kept where | Kept for | Disposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test reports | LIMS and PDF archive | 10 years | Deleted; log entry |
| Worksheets and raw data | Scanned to job file; paper for 2 years | 10 years (scan) | Paper shredded after scanning check |
| Equipment calibration and check records | Equipment file | Life of the equipment + 5 years | Deleted |
| Training and authorisation records | Personnel file | Employment + 5 years | Deleted; shredded |
| Internal audits, management reviews | Quality folder | 10 years | Deleted |
| Complaints, nonconformities, corrective actions | Quality folder | 10 years | Deleted |

**Restore test.** In March the quality manager asks IT to restore a job
folder from 2019 from backup. It takes 20 minutes and all files open. The
test is recorded.

## Common findings

- **No retention periods**, or periods shorter than the accreditation cycle.
- **Records that cannot be found** or take days to retrieve.
- **Backups never tested.**
- **Paper records damaged** or kept in unsecured places.
- **Records altered without a trace.**
