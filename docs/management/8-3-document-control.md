# 8.3 Control of documents

## In one sentence

Make sure every document people work from is approved before use, is the
current version, can be identified, and that old versions cannot be used
by mistake.

## What an assessor looks for

- **Approval before issue** by an authorised person.
- **Periodic review** and updates when needed.
- **Changes and current revision status visible**: version numbers, dates,
  and a note of what changed.
- **Current versions available where the work happens**: at the bench, in
  the instrument software, on site.
- **Unique identification** of every document.
- **Obsolete documents withdrawn**, or clearly marked if kept for reference.
- **External documents controlled too**: standards, manuals, regulations.
  Usually the assessor checks you have and use the current edition of each
  standard in your scope.

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **Keep one master list**: document ID, title, version, date, owner,
   review date, and where copies are.
2. **Use a simple ID and version scheme**: for example SOP-MO-02 rev. 3.
   Put the ID, version and page numbering ("page 2 of 6") on every page.
3. **Approve before issue.** The owner drafts; an authorised person
   approves; the approval is recorded (a signature, or an electronic
   approval).
4. **Control copies.** Electronic documents in one read-only folder are the
   easiest. Avoid printed copies; if you need them at the bench, number
   them and replace them when the document changes.
5. **Mark changes** so readers see what changed, for example a short change
   history at the end or highlighted text.
6. **Review every document regularly**, for example every two years, and
   when a related standard changes.
7. **Check standards' editions** at least once a year against the
   publisher's catalogue.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab's document master list, extract:

| ID | Title | Rev. | Date | Owner | Next review | Copies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| QM-01 | Quality manual | 5 | Jan 2026 | Quality manager | Jan 2028 | Server (read-only) |
| SOP-MO-02 | Total moisture | 3 | Mar 2025 | Technical manager | Mar 2027 | Server; printed copy no. 1 in drying room |
| SOP-AS-01 | Ash content | 2 | Jun 2024 | Technical manager | Jun 2026 | Server |
| EXT-std-ash | Standard method for ash in solid biofuels | Current edition | – | Quality manager | Yearly edition check | Licensed PDF on server |

**Edition check.** In the yearly check, the quality manager finds a new
edition of the ash standard has been published. The technical manager
reviews the changes, a verification of the changed steps is planned
([7.2](../process/7-2-methods.md)), and SOP-AS-01 is updated to rev. 3
with a change note. The old printed copy is collected and destroyed; the
old electronic version is moved to an archive folder marked "Superseded".

## Common findings

- **Old editions of standards in use**, or no check of current editions.
- **Uncontrolled printed copies** at benches or in instrument rooms.
- **Documents without version or approval.**
- **Changes not marked**, so staff miss what changed.
- **Personal copies** of methods with handwritten changes.
- **Review dates passed** without review.
