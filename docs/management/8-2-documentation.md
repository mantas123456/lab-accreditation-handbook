# 8.2 Management system documentation

## In one sentence

Write down your policies and objectives, show that top management is
committed to them, make sure every document the system needs is linked
from one place, and make sure staff can reach what applies to their work.

## What an assessor looks for

- **Policies and objectives** for meeting the standard, written down and
  communicated: what the lab aims for in competence, impartiality and
  consistent operation.
- **Commitment from top management**: evidence they support the system and
  its improvement, not only a signature on the manual.
- **All documentation referenced or linked** from the system: procedures,
  methods, forms, records, external documents.
- **Staff access** to the parts that apply to them.
- **Documents that match practice.** The assessor compares what is written
  with what people do.

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **Write a short quality manual** (10 to 20 pages is enough) with: the
   policy statement, objectives, the scope, the organisation, and a list of
   the procedures and where they are.
2. **Set objectives you can measure**: for example "no overdue corrective
   actions", "all PT results satisfactory", "reports issued within 10
   working days in 95 % of jobs". Review them at management review.
3. **Use three levels and keep them thin:**
    - Manual: the "what" and "why".
    - Procedures: who does what for the system (document control, audits,
      complaints).
    - Method SOPs and work instructions: how each test is done.
4. **Write procedures with the people who do the work**, in the words they
   use. A short procedure people follow beats a long one they don't.
5. **Include external documents**: standards, regulations, the
   accreditation body's rules, equipment manuals, in the document list.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab's document structure:

| Level | Documents | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Manual | Quality manual QM-01: policy, objectives, scope, organisation, list of procedures | Quality manager |
| Procedures | Document control, records, complaints, nonconforming work, corrective action, internal audits, management review, contract review, purchasing, training | Quality manager |
| Methods | One SOP per method in scope, written in the lab's own format, referring to the standard method | Technical manager |
| Equipment | One operating and maintenance instruction per test rig | Technical manager |
| Forms | Worksheets, receipt form, training record, audit checklist | Document owners |
| External | Standards, accreditation body rules, manuals | Quality manager |

**Objectives for this year** (from the manual): all PT z-scores satisfactory;
corrective actions closed within 60 days; turnaround of 10 working days in
95 % of jobs; each analyst authorised for at least two methods. Each is
reported at management review.

## Common findings

- **Policies with no measurable objectives**, or objectives never reviewed.
- **A manual copied from another lab** or from a consultant's template,
  describing things the lab does not do.
- **Procedures that contradict practice.**
- **External documents missing** from the system: old editions of
  standards in use, manuals not controlled.
- **Staff who cannot find** the procedure for their own task.
