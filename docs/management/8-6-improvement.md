# 8.6 Improvement

## In one sentence

Look actively for ways to do better, ask your customers what they think,
and use what you learn.

## What an assessor looks for

- **Improvement opportunities identified and acted on**, from many
  sources: audits, data analysis, QC trends, PT results, staff
  suggestions, risk assessment, management review.
- **Customer feedback asked for**, positive and negative, not only
  complaints received.
- **Feedback analysed and used** to improve the system, the lab's work and
  customer service.
- **Records**: what was improved and why.

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **Ask customers once a year.** A five-question email survey is enough:
   quality of results, clarity of reports, turnaround, communication, one
   thing to improve. Also note comments made by phone or in meetings.
2. **Keep an improvement log**: the idea, where it came from, the decision,
   the result. Anyone can add to it.
3. **Look at your own data**: turnaround times, repeat tests, QC rejects,
   PT trends. Patterns point to improvements.
4. **Pick a few improvements a year** and finish them. Five finished beat
   twenty started.
5. **Report at management review**
   ([8.9](8-9-management-review.md)): feedback received, improvements made,
   results.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab sends a survey to its 25 regular customers; 14 reply.

| Question | Average (1 = poor, 5 = excellent) |
|---|---|
| Confidence in results | 4.6 |
| Clarity of reports | 3.5 |
| Turnaround time | 3.9 |
| Ease of contact | 4.4 |

Comments on reports: "I don't understand what ± means", "Which basis is
this, as received or dry?"

**Improvements logged:**

| Improvement | Source | Action | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Report clarity | Survey | New report template: basis stated in every row; short note explaining the uncertainty; a summary table first | Next survey: clarity 4.3 |
| Turnaround of calorific value tests | Own data: 14 days average | Calorimeter runs grouped twice a week instead of weekly | Average 9 days |
| Fewer repeat ash tests | QC trend: crucibles cracking | New crucible supplier | Repeats halved |

## Common findings

- **No customer feedback sought**, only complaints recorded.
- **Feedback collected but not analysed** or not acted on.
- **Improvement exists only as corrective action**: the lab only improves
  after something goes wrong.
- **No record** of improvements made.
