# 8.5 Risks and opportunities

## In one sentence

Think ahead about what could stop your lab producing valid results and what
could help it do better, act on the important ones in proportion to their
effect, and check whether the actions worked.

## What an assessor looks for

- **Risks and opportunities identified** that relate to the lab's
  activities, not only general business risks.
- **Planned actions** for them, built into the management system (into
  procedures, training, equipment plans), not only listed.
- **Proportion**: the effort matches the possible effect on the validity
  of results. A formal risk management method is not required.
- **A check of effectiveness**: did the action reduce the risk?
- **Links**: risks feed into management review, and corrective actions
  update the risks ([8.7](8-7-corrective-action.md)).

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **Hold a one-hour session** with the whole team once a year. Ask:
   *What could make a result wrong, late or lost? What could make us
   better?* Use the lab's processes as a checklist: contract review,
   sampling, receipt, testing, QC, reporting, equipment, people, IT.
2. **Rate each risk simply**: likelihood (low, medium, high) and effect on
   results (low, medium, high).
3. **Act on the high ones first.** The action should change something real:
   a check, a backup, a second trained person.
4. **Write the action into the system**: the SOP, the equipment plan, the
   training matrix.
5. **Review at management review**: is the risk lower? Any new risks from
   changes, complaints or nonconformities?
6. **Record opportunities too**, such as new methods customers ask for,
   automation, or a cheaper PT scheme that covers more methods.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab's risk register, extract:

| Risk or opportunity | Likelihood | Effect | Action | Check of effectiveness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Only one analyst authorised for CHNS analysis | High (staff leave) | High (method unavailable) | Train a second analyst by June | Second analyst authorised in May ✓ |
| Spreadsheet calculation error | Medium | High | Validate all calculation sheets; lock formulas ([7.11](../process/7-11-data-management.md)) | Validation done; one error found and fixed ✓ |
| Single drying oven fails | Low | High | Agreement to use a partner lab's oven | Agreement signed; not yet needed |
| Moisture samples change in transit | Medium | Medium | Airtight containers sent to customers; condition checked at receipt | Fewer "condition doubtful" notes this year ✓ |
| *Opportunity:* customers ask for chlorine analysis | – | – | Evaluate in-house method vs. subcontracting | Decision at management review |

The impartiality risks ([4.1](../general/4-1-impartiality.md)) are kept in a
separate section of the same register.

## Common findings

- **A register of general business risks** (fire, finances) with nothing
  about valid results.
- **Risks listed with no actions**, or actions that are never checked.
- **The register never updated** after complaints, nonconformities or
  changes.
- **Over-engineered systems** with scores and matrices nobody uses.
