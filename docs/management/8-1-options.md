# 8.1 Option A or option B

## In one sentence

Run a management system that supports everything else in the standard,
either by meeting the management clauses directly (option A) or by
showing that an existing ISO 9001 system already covers them (option B).

## What an assessor looks for

- **A clear choice**, stated in your quality manual or equivalent: option A
  or option B.
- **Option A**: the system covers, at least, documentation
  ([8.2](8-2-documentation.md)), document control
  ([8.3](8-3-document-control.md)), records ([8.4](8-4-records.md)), risks
  and opportunities ([8.5](8-5-risks-and-opportunities.md)), improvement
  ([8.6](8-6-improvement.md)), corrective action
  ([8.7](8-7-corrective-action.md)), internal audits
  ([8.8](8-8-internal-audits.md)) and management review
  ([8.9](8-9-management-review.md)).
- **Option B**: the lab runs within an ISO 9001 system. In practice that
  system must also do the jobs clauses 8.2 to 8.9 describe, and must help
  the lab meet clauses 4 to 7 reliably. ISO 9001 certification is not
  required, but the system has to work.
- **Either way, a system that works**: the assessor looks at what the
  system does, not only at what the manual says.

## How to do it in a small lab

**Choose option A** when the lab stands alone or its parent has no ISO 9001
system. It is the simpler path: one system, built for the lab.

**Choose option B** when the lab is part of an organisation that already
runs ISO 9001. Then:

1. Map the 17025 clauses 8.2 to 8.9 against the parent's procedures, and
   write down where each one is met.
2. Make sure the parent's internal audits and management reviews include
   the lab's technical requirements (clauses 4 to 7), not only the
   general quality system.
3. Fill gaps with lab-specific procedures, for example impartiality and
   technical record keeping.
4. Be ready to show the assessor the parent's documents and records that
   cover the lab.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab sits in a research institute certified to ISO 9001.

| 17025 clause | Met by | Gap? |
|---|---|---|
| 8.2 Documentation | Institute quality manual + lab manual | Lab manual adds impartiality and confidentiality policies |
| 8.3 Document control | Institute document system | None |
| 8.4 Records | Institute records procedure | Lab adds technical record rules ([7.5](../process/7-5-technical-records.md)) |
| 8.5 Risks | Institute risk register | Lab section added for risks to the validity of results |
| 8.6, 8.7 | Institute improvement and corrective action procedure | None |
| 8.8 Internal audits | Institute audit programme | Lab audits added for clauses 4–7, by a technically competent auditor |
| 8.9 Management review | Institute review | Lab review added, with the 17025 inputs |

The lab chooses **option B** and records the mapping in its manual.

## Common findings

- **Option B claimed**, but the ISO 9001 audits and reviews never look at
  the lab's technical work.
- **No statement** of which option the lab uses.
- **Option A system on paper only**: procedures written, but no audits,
  reviews or corrective actions actually happening.
