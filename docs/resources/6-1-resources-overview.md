# 6.1 Resources overview

## In one sentence

Have the people, rooms, equipment, systems and outside services each
activity needs, and be able to show that you have them.

## What an assessor looks for

This short clause opens Part 6. The detail is in the clauses that follow:

| Resource | Clause |
|---|---|
| People and their competence | [6.2 Personnel](6-2-personnel.md) |
| Rooms and environmental conditions | [6.3 Facilities](6-3-facilities.md) |
| Instruments, software, reference materials, reagents | [6.4 Equipment](6-4-equipment.md) |
| The link from your results to SI units | [6.5 Traceability](6-5-traceability.md) |
| Calibration services, suppliers, subcontractors | [6.6 External providers](6-6-external-providers.md) |

What the assessor checks at this level is simple: **for every activity in
your scope, are all the resources there?** A method in your scope with only
one authorised analyst who has left, or an instrument out of service for
months, is a gap.

## How to do it in a small lab

1. **Make a resource map** for each method in your scope: the people
   authorised for it, the room, the equipment, the reference materials,
   the software and any outside service it depends on.
2. **Look for single points of failure.** One authorised analyst, one
   instrument with no backup, one supplier for a critical reagent. Decide
   what you will do if that one thing is missing.
3. **Review the map** at management review
   ([8.9](../management/8-9-management-review.md)), and when people,
   equipment or scope change.
4. **Do not keep a method in scope** that you cannot actually run. Suspend
   it with your accreditation body if a resource is gone for a long time.

## Worked example

A made-up biofuel lab's resource map for its total moisture test:

| Resource | What the test needs | In place | Single point of failure? |
|---|---|---|---|
| People | Analysts authorised for moisture | C and E | No |
| Facilities | Drying room with ventilation; balance room, stable temperature | Rooms 1 and 2 | No |
| Equipment | Drying oven at 105 °C; balance; trays; reference tray | Oven O-01, balance B-03 | **Yes: one oven** |
| Traceability | Balance calibration; oven thermometer calibration | Certificates on file | No |
| Reference material | Control sample for QC | Pellet control batch, 18 months left | No |
| Software | Moisture calculation spreadsheet v2.1 | Validated | No |
| External services | Balance and thermometer calibration supplier | Approved supplier | No |

**Action.** The single oven is a risk. The lab agrees with a nearby
accredited lab to use its oven if O-01 fails, and records that the oven
there must be checked with the lab's own calibrated thermometer before
use.

## Common findings

- **Methods in scope that cannot be run** because a person or instrument
  is gone.
- **No backup** for a key person or instrument, and no plan.
- **Resources listed but not checked**: equipment on the list that is out
  of service or out of calibration.
