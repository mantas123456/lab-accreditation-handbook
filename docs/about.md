# About

## Who wrote it

I'm Mantas Valantinavicius. I took part in one full ISO/IEC 17025
accreditation, for solid biofuel and SRF (solid recovered fuel) testing,
and helped maintain ISO/IEC 17020 and ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation in the
same lab. I also write [labqc](https://github.com/mantas123456/lab-quality-toolkit),
a Python library for lab quality calculations.

## Why

When I went through accreditation, the standard told us what to do but not
how a small lab could actually do it. The useful knowledge lived in
assessors' comments, in colleagues' heads and in old procedures. This
handbook collects that practical side in one free place, with templates
you can edit and tools that do the calculations.

## What it is, and what it is not

- It is a **practical interpretation** written in my own words, with
  made-up examples based on real kinds of tests.
- It is **not official guidance** from ISO, ILAC, EA or any accreditation
  body. It never reproduces the text of the standard. The standard and
  your accreditation body's documents always take priority.
- No examples use real clients, samples, results or internal documents
  from any lab.
