# Accreditation in Malta

## Who accredits labs in Malta

**NAB-Malta** is Malta's national accreditation body. It accredits testing
and calibration laboratories to ISO/IEC 17025, as well as other
conformity assessment bodies. Under EU rules there is one national
accreditation body per member state, so a lab based in Malta applies to
NAB-Malta.

Its website is **[nabmalta.org.mt](https://nabmalta.org.mt)**.

!!! warning "Check NAB-Malta's current documents"
    This page deliberately does not state fees, timelines, application
    forms or policies, because they change. Before you plan, read NAB-Malta's
    current documents: the application process, the rules for using the
    accreditation mark, its policies on proficiency testing and
    traceability, and its fee schedule. Where NAB-Malta's rules and this
    handbook differ, NAB-Malta's rules apply.

## What to find out early

Questions worth asking NAB-Malta (or finding in its documents) before you
apply:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Which documents must go with the application? | Sets what you must have ready |
| Does it offer a pre-assessment visit? | A useful readiness check |
| What does it expect on proficiency testing: how often, which schemes? | PT takes months to arrange |
| What are its rules on traceability of calibrations? | Affects which calibration providers you can use |
| How does it grade findings, and what are the deadlines to close them? | Plan time after the assessment |
| How long is the accreditation cycle, and how often are surveillance visits? | Plan for the years after |
| Are technical assessors available for your field, or brought in from abroad? | May affect timing |

## Practical points for a small island market

- **Calibration and PT providers are often abroad.** Allow for shipping
  time, customs and the risk of damage when you plan calibrations and PT
  rounds. Choose providers accredited by a body that is a signatory to the
  European or international agreements, so their certificates are
  accepted ([6.5](../resources/6-5-traceability.md),
  [6.6](../resources/6-6-external-providers.md)).
- **Backup capacity is limited.** With few labs doing the same tests, plan
  for instrument failure early, for example with an agreement with a lab
  abroad ([6.1](../resources/6-1-resources-overview.md)).
- **Climate matters.** Heat and humidity affect balances, drying ovens and
  calorimetry. Monitor room conditions closely
  ([6.3](../resources/6-3-facilities.md)).
