# 1. Confidential information

Confidential information means anything I see, hear or receive while
working for the laboratory about its customers or their work, including:
samples, results, reports, drawings, product details, prices, contracts,
and the fact that a customer uses the laboratory at all.

# 2. What I agree to

1. I will use confidential information only for the laboratory's work.
2. I will not disclose it to anyone outside the laboratory, or to anyone
   inside who does not need it for their work.
3. I will not take photos of samples, equipment under test or documents
   unless my work requires it and the customer has agreed.
4. I will keep paper and electronic information secure: locked away,
   password-protected, and never on personal devices or accounts unless the
   laboratory allows it.
5. If I am asked for information by anyone outside the laboratory,
   including authorities, I will pass the request to the quality manager
   and not answer myself.
6. These duties continue after I leave the laboratory.

# 3. Impartiality

1. My results and opinions will not be influenced by commercial, financial
   or other pressure, from customers, colleagues or management.
2. I will tell the quality manager at once if anyone puts pressure on me
   about a result.
3. I will not accept gifts or favours from customers or suppliers beyond
   what the laboratory's policy allows.

# 4. Declaration of interests

I will declare any interest that could affect, or appear to affect, my
work: for example employment, ownership, family ties or consulting for a
customer, supplier or competitor. I will update this declaration when
something changes.
