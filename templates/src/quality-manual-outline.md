# 1. About the laboratory

*Legal entity, sites, history in two sentences, and what the lab does.*

# 2. Policy statement

*Signed by top management. In your own words, commit to: competent work,
impartiality, consistent operation, meeting ISO/IEC 17025 and customer
requirements, and improving the system. Keep it to half a page.*

# 3. Objectives

*Measurable, reviewed at management review.*

| Objective | Measure | Target | Reviewed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valid results | PT z-scores satisfactory | 100 % | Yearly |
| Timely reports | Reports within agreed turnaround | 95 % | Yearly |
| Effective corrective action | Corrective actions closed on time | 90 % | Yearly |
